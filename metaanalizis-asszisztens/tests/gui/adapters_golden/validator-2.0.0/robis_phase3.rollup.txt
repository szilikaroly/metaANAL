  Implied overall: HIGH / SERIOUS — Phase 3, the overall judgement; concerns in Domain 1, Domain 2 feed it, they do not set it.
  ROBIS rates domains 1-4 as concerns that inform phase 3; the overall risk of bias is the phase-3 judgement, not the worst domain (Whiting 2016, PMC4687950, section 3.3).
  The three tiers in ROBIS's own terms: low = Low, middle = Unclear, high = High.
